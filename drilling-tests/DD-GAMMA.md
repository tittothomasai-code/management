# DD Training natural gamma log

Open the game and choose **Drill → DD Training → Start**. The read-only log appears in the run panel. Open **Gamma log and depth references** for the last 60 stations, source values and model interval. DD Tracks uses the same run panel. Pause holds the display. Reset, loading another well plan, or launching a track creates a fresh detector, depth buffer and filter. No estimator output feeds steering, scoring, drilling time, or the motor.

## Depth and source

All distances are metres. The DD exercise supplies segment endpoints along its drilled path. A virtual LWD tool samples every 1 m of bit MD with a fixed 6 m along-hole setback. The sensor first has coverage after 7 m of new hole. A station pairs gamma with the mechanical sample stored when the bit passed that exact MD. Sensor TVD, North and East come from interpolation between the surrounding drilled endpoints. Latest survey MD/TVD and current bit MD have independent labels. The run has no independent hole-depth channel. The estimate applies at sensor MD, leaving the interval ahead of the detector unobserved.

Only the built-in synthetic earth model supplies the virtual log. The raw gamma sample adds seeded detector noise to the game's natural gamma response at the sensor position. The original run ROP stays visible. The DD run calls its motor/ROP law with reference formation factor 1. The logging instrument calls that same ROP law with actual WOB (t), bit RPM and the synthetic bed's drillability factor, plus 2% relative noise, to make a separate **synthetic formation-response ROP** (m/h). Flow (L/min) must be positive for mechanical evidence. Differential pressure and torque both reflect motor loading in this model and provide no independent gamma observation. The filter receives the two ROP values and mode. It never receives formation names or gamma truth.

## Estimator and tuning

The latent scalar is local natural gamma response in gAPI. A depth-domain random walk predicts the previous mean and increases its variance by 5 gAPI² per metre. A change in log(formation-response ROP / reference ROP) at adjacent, mode-compatible sensor stations adds up to 240 gAPI² of process variance, proportional to the squared contrast divided by 0.18 squared, capped at one. That gives gamma more weight near a change in drilling response. The mechanical channel contributes no signed gamma increment.

The gamma observation has unit gain. Its synthetic variance is 3² + 7² × (acquisition ROP / 12 m/h) × mode factor, in gAPI². Acquisition ROP is distance divided by elapsed drilling time within the 1 m bin. The fixed depth gate has less counting time at higher ROP. The factor is one in rotate and two in slide. These constants approximate depth-gated detector noise for gameplay. They have no field calibration. The displayed approximate 95% band is ±1.96 posterior standard deviations, conditional on this model.

The raw sample always remains visible, including a flagged spike. An innovation beyond 3.5 predicted standard deviations is held. Two consistent adjacent excursions can start a new level. A dropout or invalid gamma grows uncertainty with drilled distance. A packet arriving over 120 simulated seconds after acquisition, a repeated or reordered station, and inconsistent sensor MD cannot change the estimate. Off-bottom, connection, stall and mixed-regime observations are gated. DD Training represents pickup, stall, and stopped penetration explicitly. It has no timed pipe-connection channel. Unobserved gaps in the drilled path restart sensor coverage. No external telemetry, wall-clock aging of a paused game, or lithology classification is used.

Model assumptions are in `drilling/dd_gamma_filter.mjs` and the virtual instrument in `drilling/dd_gamma_log.mjs`. Setback must be a whole number of logging intervals. Run `node --test drilling-tests/dd-gamma.test.mjs` and `node drilling-tests/dd-gamma-browser.mjs` from the repository root. The browser check uses the local game entry and blocks external requests.

Set `ROBOTYX_GAME_ENTRY_HTML` to a downloaded public game index to run the same offline browser check with that entry markup. The 390 px check uses touch events.
