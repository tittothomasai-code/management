Directional Drilling Simulation

  1. Choose 10 m, 30 m or tally spacing.
  2. Drag any future ring. It becomes a fixed target, and the guide rings align along the new curve.
  3. Orbit on empty sky. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Use the coloured N/E/TVD arrows, coordinates or drag modes for precise 3D placement. Select Landing to resize its sweet-zone rectangle, set entry heading, or follow the bed dip. Focus the target to drag its white corners.
  4. Start the run. Move a future ring while flying; the remaining path changes smoothly from the bit. The flown trail stays fixed.
  5. Open Reservoir for roof/floor controls. Enable two-finger shaping: vertical motion changes depth, pinch changes thickness, twist changes dip. The simulation stops a route at its first floor conflict.
  6. Choose rolling, dome, basin, saddle or folded surfaces and adjust relief / N–E scale. Tap a surface point or select XYZ to read East / North / TVD; choose roof or floor for a specific layer.
  7. Try Follow bit for a chase camera. Green rings are cleared. Restart begins at your chosen kickoff.

This is a simulation using a smooth spline. It does not certify BHA response, DLS feasibility, collision clearance or a real well plan. It reads no live feed or account data. The bit is an enlarged display model.
