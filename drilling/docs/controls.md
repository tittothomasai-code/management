# On-screen controls (v5.9.5, SIM)
- A small 🎮 button sits at the bottom right. Tap it to show or hide the on-screen controls.
- The controls are OFF by default. They never turn on by themselves on touch devices.
- Your choice is saved on this device (localStorage `dw_vpad`). Button group position is saved in `dw_vpad_pos`.
- You can also turn them on or off in Tools ▸ Settings ▸ "On-screen controls".
- Left stick: orbit the camera. Right stick: zoom (up/down) and look (left/right).
- Buttons: WOB +/−, RPM +/−, fly to bit, reset view. Drag the grip to move the button group.
- The controls are semi-transparent. They hide while full-screen panels are open (EDM import, start screen, EDM filters).
- Gamepad and keyboard support (input.js) stays the same. The on-screen controls use the same actions.
